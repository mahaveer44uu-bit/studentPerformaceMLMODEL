TEST 3: Minimum Attendance (Important!)

"अब मैं 30% attendance देता हूं..."

[30% enter करें]

"System ने reject किया: 'Minimum 45% attendance required'

[यहां explain करें:]

बोलें: "Sir, यह हमारी design decision है क्योंकि:

 1. Educational Policy:
    → Most institutions 75% minimum require करती हैं
    → 45% से कम means majority classes missed
 
 2. ML Model Domain:
    → हमारी training data 45-100% range में है
    → Out-of-distribution data unreliable होगा
 
 3. Practical Reasoning:
    → इतनी कम attendance में prediction meaningless है
    → Student को intervention चाहिए, prediction नहीं
 
 4. Responsible AI:
    → जब accurate prediction नहीं दे सकते, तो
      better है system warn करे
 
 यह quality over quantity approach है."
